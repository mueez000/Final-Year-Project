1.2 Problem Statement
Pakistanâ€™s motorway network, particularly the 
M-2
, faces recurring safety issues especially in the Salt Range where more than 350 accidents have occurred since 1997, claiming over 
200 lives
. The existing systems (manual speed monitoring, convoy system, manual incident reporting, and limited speed cameras) are inefficient, reactive, labor-intensive, cause traffic congestion, allow easy rule evasion, and lack real-time monitoring, drowsiness detection, automatic crash alerts, and centralized analytics.
1.3 Proposed Solution
The proposed 
AI-Powered Intelligent Vehicle Monitoring System (IVMS)
 is a comprehensive smartphone-based solution using Flutter and Firebase. It enables real-time monitoring of commercial buses and vans, AI-based drowsiness &amp; crash detection, speed violation alerts, automated e-challans, instant SOS, officer response management, and headquarters analytics. The system will significantly improve road safety, reduce response time, and replace the inefficient convoy system with intelligent virtual enforcement.

5 Non-Functional Requirements
5.1 Security
The system shall use secure authentication mechanisms.
All sensitive data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.
Role-based access control shall be implemented.
Evidence shall be timestamped, geo-tagged, and hash-verified.
Explicit consent shall be mandatory for data collection.
5.2 Usability
The mobile app shall have simple and intuitive interface suitable for drivers.
Audio feedback shall be provided for important alerts.
The web dashboard shall be user-friendly for control room environment.
5.3 Performance
Real-time location updates shall have low latency.
AI/ML processing shall be fast and battery efficient.
The system shall support multiple concurrent users efficiently.
5.4 Flexibility
The system shall allow HQ to configure speed limits, hazard zones, and AI parameters easily.
Architecture shall support future expansion to other motorways.
5.5 Responsiveness
The application shall be responsive on different mobile devices and screen sizes.
The web dashboard shall work well on desktop monitors.
5.6 Availability
The system shall work in offline mode with automatic synchronization.
Critical SOS and crash alerts shall be highly reliable.
